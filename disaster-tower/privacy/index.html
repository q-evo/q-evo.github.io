Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Disaster Tower (the "game") is made by Matthew Brown, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email support@thebuddysystem.dev.

The short version
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no profiles.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, towers, bolts, toppers and settings) is saved only on your device. I never receive it.
  • I don't use any analytics or tracking tools of my own.
  • The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage information to show and measure those ads.
  • You decide whether ads may be personalized, and you can change your mind at any time.

1. Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress on your iPhone using Apple's standard app storage: the levels you've completed, your stars and best costs, the towers you've designed, your bolts and toppers, your Sandbox setup, and your settings such as sound and haptics. This information stays on your device and is never sent to me or to anyone else by the game.

It may be included in your iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. You can erase it at any time with Settings, Reset progress in the game, or by deleting the app.

2. Advertising by Google AdMob

Ads in the game are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited). An ad may appear when you move on from a level you have beaten. To request, show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads software built into the game may collect:

  • Device identifiers: Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV).
  • Network and approximate location: your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location, such as your country or city.
  • Device and app information: such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown and whether you tapped or watched them.
  • Diagnostics: such as crash information and how long ads take to load.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure how they perform, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and abuse, and, if you allow it, personalize ads based on your activity in other apps and websites. Google also uses Google's consent software (the User Messaging Platform) to ask for and remember your choices where the law requires it.

Ads are limited to content rated as suitable for general audiences. If you ever see an ad that seems inappropriate, use Report an ad in the game's settings or email me, and I'll block it. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Your choices

Tracking permission. The first time you open the game, iOS asks whether Disaster Tower may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your IDFA is not shared. You will still see ads, but they won't be personalized using your activity elsewhere. You can change this at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Consent in Europe and the UK. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown or information is stored on your device for advertising. You can review or change those choices at any time in the game under Settings, Privacy and cookie settings.

Playing offline. The game works without an internet connection. When you're offline, no ads are requested and nothing is sent to Google.

4. Information I receive

None from the game itself. Disaster Tower has no accounts, no chat, and no analytics or crash reporting services of my own.

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and I use it only to reply to you. If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your iPhone's Analytics & Improvements settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with me. Apple provides these under its own privacy policy, and they don't identify you.

I don't sell your personal information, and I don't receive any personal information from Google about the people who see ads in the game.

5. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including in the EEA, the UK and US states such as California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of personalized advertising (sometimes called the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information).

  • To stop ads from being personalized, choose Ask App Not to Track, or turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track in your iPhone settings. In Europe and the UK you can also use Privacy and cookie settings in the game.
  • To delete the game's data, use Reset progress in the game's settings or delete the app.
  • For information Google holds, you can use the tools in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • For anything else, email me and I'll help. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

In the EEA and the UK, ad data is processed on the basis of your consent, given through Google's consent message, or Google's legitimate interests in measuring ads and preventing fraud, as described in that message.

6. Children

Disaster Tower is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you're a parent or guardian and have questions, please email me.

7. Retention and security

Your game data stays on your device until you reset it or delete the app. I don't keep copies of it on any server. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention practices. The game communicates with Google's ad servers over encrypted HTTPS connections, and Google may process this information in the United States and other countries where it operates.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, I'll also mention it in the game's App Store release notes.
