Common questions

How do I build a tower?

Drag from any bolt to place a beam, then let go on another bolt to connect them. The circle around your finger shows how long the current material can reach. When you're happy with your tower, tap the play button to unleash the disaster.

My tower keeps folding over. What am I doing wrong?

Squares fold like cardboard. Add diagonal beams so every square becomes two triangles, because triangles can't change shape. Level 1 walks you through it.

How do I find the weak spots?

During a test, beams glow yellow and then red as they get close to breaking. While building, tap the gauge icon to preview the stress from gravity and the payload. Every level plays out exactly the same way each time, so you can rebuild the weak part and try again.

Which material should I use?

Wood is cheap and light but burns. Steel is strong and conducts lightning. Concrete is heavy and great at holding weight but weak when pulled. Cables only pull, which makes them perfect guy wires and lightning rods. Long beams buckle more easily than short ones.

Lightning keeps setting my tower on fire.

Lightning strikes the highest point. Give it a continuous path of steel or cable down to a foundation and it will be grounded safely instead of igniting the wood.

How do stars, bolts and toppers work?

You earn more stars by beating a level for less money. Winning also pays out bolts: 60 for the first clear, 40 for each new star, and 10 when you beat your best cost by at least 5%. Spend bolts in Toppers on the main menu to decorate your tower with flags, a rubber duck, a garden gnome and more. Toppers are just for fun and don't change the physics.

I'm stuck. Can I skip ahead?

Yes. Open Settings (the gear on the main menu) and turn on Unlock all levels. You can also try any combination of disasters in Sandbox.

Why do I see ads?

Disaster Tower is free, and ads keep it that way. An ad may play when you move on from a level you've beaten. Ads never interrupt a build or a test, never appear after the first level, and show at most once every 45 seconds.

I saw an inappropriate ad. How do I report it?

Sorry about that. Tap Report an ad in the game's Settings, or email support@thebuddysystem.dev. Tell me what the ad showed (a screenshot is perfect) and I'll block it.

How do I control ad tracking and consent?

Whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier is up to you: on your iPhone, go to Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you can review your ad consent choices any time in the game under Settings, Privacy and cookie settings. The privacy policy has the details.

Is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your iPhone and is included in your normal iCloud or computer backups, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings it along. It doesn't sync live between devices.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap Reset progress. This erases your stars and saved towers. Bolts and toppers are reset too.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. You can build and test towers offline. Ads need a connection, so offline play simply has no ads.
